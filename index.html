Important Legal Disclaimer

TAG PRO AGENCY LLC is not a law firm, and our staff members are not attorneys. We do not provide legal advice, select legal forms, explain legal rights, represent customers in court, or guarantee any application outcome.

We provide non-attorney administrative assistance using information and customer-selected forms supplied by the customer, including typing, translation, notarization, application support, printing, scanning, and document submission.

Wills, divorce documents, Powers of Attorney, and other legal forms must be selected or prepared by the customer or a licensed attorney. Our notary may notarize eligible signatures but cannot prepare legal documents or provide legal advice. For legal advice or representation, consult a licensed attorney.
